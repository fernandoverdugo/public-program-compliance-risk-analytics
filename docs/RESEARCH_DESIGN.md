# Research Design

## Business problem

Regulated public programs must monitor many providers with limited review capacity.

## Primary question

Which operational, reporting, complaint, inspection, and remediation characteristics are associated with future material compliance findings?

## Unit of analysis

Provider-month.

## Primary outcome

`future_material_finding`: whether a provider receives a material compliance finding within the next 90 days.

## Responsible interpretation

Risk scores prioritize human review. They do not establish noncompliance or automate enforcement.
